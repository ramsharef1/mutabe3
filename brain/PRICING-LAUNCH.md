# PRICING-LAUNCH — mutabe3
Status: approved with plan v2 (D-050). Prices are **set by the seller outside the system**; this file gives the method and the 90-day plan.

## Rate-card method (numbers to be set by Rami/the client)
1. **Unit of sale** = zone × position × device × week, flat weekly price. Public reference for the structure (not the level): [GDN 2026 rate card](https://www.gdnonline.com/pdf/GDN_2026_rate_card.pdf) — 728×90 home leaderboard BD 500/week, 300×250 BD 400/week, 15% agency commission.
2. **Share of voice:** a zone rotates up to 6 banners, so a rotation slot ≈ exclusive price ÷ n, plus an exclusivity premium.
3. **Sanity check:** CPM-equivalent = weekly price ÷ (counted impressions ÷ 1000); compare with the buyer's own Meta quote for Jordan. Requires the impression rollups (weeks 3–4).
4. **Sponsored article** = writing hours × rate + promotion bundle + premium.
5. Series discounts; agency commission policy.
No Jordanian outlet publishes prices ([Khaberni](https://www.khaberni.com/page/ad-with-us), [Saraya](https://www.sarayanews.com/pages/adv), [Al-Ghad](https://alghad.com/اعلن-معنا) list contacts only; [Dar Al-Sharq](https://alsharq.net.sa/adv/) lists packages without prices).

## Payments
Bank transfer / CliQ ([JoPACC](https://www.jopacc.com/what-we-do/services-products/cliq-services)); invoices through the seller's JoFotara account ([mandatory since 2025-04-01](https://www.cleartax.com/jo/jordan-e-invoicing)). **The site records nothing about money** (owner decision 4.3). **Counsel:** which entity invoices, sales-tax registration, and whether ads may be sold before the licence is granted.

## Platform tiers (for later newsroom clients; prices TBD)
Site (own instance, hosting, updates) · Newsroom (+ ads manager, newsletter) · Managed (+ ops/SEO). Setup fee + monthly fee; JOD in Jordan, USD elsewhere. Deferred until the first sale.
