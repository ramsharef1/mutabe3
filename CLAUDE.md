## Overview

Mutabe3 is a full-stack news agency website for Jordan. The platform enables journalists to publish articles, readers to discover news by category/topic, and provides comprehensive editorial and analytics tools.

## Project Scope

- **Frontend:** Modern, responsive web interface with RTL/Arabic support
- **Backend:** Article management, publishing workflow, user engagement
- **Admin Panel:** Editorial dashboard for journalists and editors
- **CMS:** Content publishing and scheduling
- **Analytics:** Reader engagement and traffic metrics
- **Localization:** Arabic (primary) + English support
- **Mobile:** Fully responsive design

## Key Principles

1. **SEO-First:** News content must be discoverable and rank well
2. **Performance:** Fast load times critical for news readers
3. **RTL Ready:** Proper Arabic RTL layout from the start
4. **Editorial Workflow:** Support multi-author, review, and scheduling
5. **Analytics-Driven:** Track engagement and reader behavior
6. **Scalable:** Handle traffic spikes during major news events
